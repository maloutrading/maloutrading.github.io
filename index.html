philosophy

Pure discretionary macro

inspired by Jesse Livermore, Mark Minervini & Ed Seykota

Basically all finance professionals today agree that speculating mostly based on experience and gut feeling is "not how you do it". Also, they mostly agree that in order to achieve outsized returns one must do something that most others don’t. Quant-oriented approaches have dominated Wall Street for the last 50 years and will continue to do so. I think human judgement has gotten a little bit of a bad reputation and since Ed Seykota is right in claiming that all trading is ultimately discretionary, I consider it a feature, not a bug, that the alpaca turtle wikifolio’s trading system is pure discretionary global macro.

philosophy

Buy strength. Ride the trend. Let the stop decide.

turtle-style systematic trend following

Thirty leveraged ETFs, one rule: buy the strongest of the pack the moment it breaks its seven-day high — at most three positions, each guarded by a stop that only ever tightens. No forecasts, no targets, no opinions: losses are cut small and automatically, winners run until the trend itself ends. The system loses often and small, wins rarely and big — and when nothing is breaking out, cash is the position.
